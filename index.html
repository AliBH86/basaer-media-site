Basaer Media

Basaer Media is an independent, non-commercial project based in Bahrain. We share verses of the Holy Quran in Arabic with an English translation, recited by well-known reciters, as images, short videos and full-surah recitation videos.

The Quran text, translation and recitation audio come from the Quran.com / Quran Foundation API. Translation: Saheeh International.
