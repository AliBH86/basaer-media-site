Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Basaer Media (بصائر ميديا) is an independent, non-commercial project based in Bahrain. It shares verses of the Holy Quran in Arabic with an English translation on Instagram (@basaer.media.bh), its Facebook Page and YouTube (@Basaermedia_bh). This policy explains what our publishing tool does with data. In short: we do not collect personal data about you.

What our tool does

Our publishing tool is used only by the owner of the Basaer Media accounts. It publishes our own posts and videos to our own Instagram account and Facebook Page, and uploads our own videos to our own YouTube channel. Nobody else can sign in to it, and it does not act on any other account.

YouTube API Services

Our tool uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to our own channel, set their titles, descriptions and thumbnails, set their privacy status, and add them to our playlists. By watching our videos on YouTube you are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

The tool accesses only our own channel's data needed for those actions. It does not read, store or share data about viewers, subscribers or commenters. The account owner can revoke the tool's access at any time at Google security settings.

Data we process

  • Our own accounts: the access tokens and account identifiers of our own Instagram, Facebook Page and YouTube channel, used only to publish our own content and check that the connection works. They are kept in our automation server's encrypted credential store, never in files or messages.
  • Our own content: the images, videos, titles and captions we publish. The Quran text, translation and recitation audio come from the Quran.com / Quran Foundation API; no personal data is sent to it.
  • Nothing about you: the tool does not read, store or analyse comments, messages, followers, subscribers, likes, or any information about people who view or interact with our accounts. This website uses no cookies, tracking or advertising.

Service providers

To make and publish our content we use: Meta (Instagram, Facebook), Google (YouTube), a DigitalOcean server running our automation software, GitHub (video rendering), Cloudinary (storage of the media we publish), Telegram (private review messages to the account owner), Anthropic (drafting of caption text) and ElevenLabs (voice for poems). These services receive only our own content and account details, not data about you. Each has its own privacy policy.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share personal data. We have none to share.

Data retention

Our published media stays on Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and Cloudinary for as long as it is online. Draft records (the text of a post and its approval status) are kept on our server for our own review history. None of these records contain information about other people.

Data deletion

Because our tool stores no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete. If you believe we hold any data about you, send a direct message to @basaer.media.bh on Instagram with the words "Data deletion". We will reply within 30 days, delete anything found, and confirm.

Children

Our content is suitable for a general audience. Our tool does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Direct message on Instagram: @basaer.media.bh
